Meet Vastra.in

Vastra.in was founded by Pushpendra Waxar as a new initiative focused on saris and online clothing. It begins with a simple belief: fashion is all about clothing, and clothing can be a powerful way to express who we are.

Our vision is to make finding clothing feel personal and enjoyable. We bring together saris and online clothing, drawing inspiration from the colors, forms, and stories that make each look meaningful.
